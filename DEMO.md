# 2-minute demo script

Split screen: BAND room on the left, http://127.0.0.1:5055/soc on the right (4 dark diyas, 0 / 4).

0:00 to 0:15. "Security teams are a relay race: pentesters find, engineers fix, pentesters re-check. Diya SOC runs that relay with two agents in one BAND room, with Lyzr brains."
0:15 to 0:30. In BAND, type `@Red attack the shop`. The room goal appears. Red's exploit tool calls stream into the room. The SOC feed goes red: 4 RED HITs.
0:30 to 0:50. Red posts its Lyzr severity report and opens 4 tasks on the BAND board, then @mentions Blue.
0:50 to 1:20. Blue moves the tasks to in progress, posts root cause + fix + detection rule from its Lyzr brain, and deploys. The diyas light up one by one. Tasks move to in review, Blue @mentions Red: RETEST.
1:20 to 1:45. Red re-attacks: 4 BLOCKED in the feed, tasks close as completed, Red tells the human "all 4 exploits blocked".
1:45 to 2:00. Show the task board history and BAND memory (Blue's fix playbook). "Two agents, one room, every step visible and auditable. Happy Diwali."
